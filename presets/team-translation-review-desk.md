# Translation review desk

Coordinate Translation specialist, Document specialist, Evidence checker for translation review desk.

## Specialist roles

- [Translation specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/translation.md): Translation and localization
- [Document specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/document.md): Reports, proposals and PDFs
- [Evidence checker](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/evidence.md): Source verification

## Review and handoff

Reviewer: **QA reviewer**. The workflow uses bounded assignments, review and final synthesis. The preset can be saved as a configurable private team; remote updates do not overwrite a user’s saved copy. Tool access and model eligibility remain separate requirements.
