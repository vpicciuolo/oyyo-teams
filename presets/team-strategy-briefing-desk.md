# Strategy briefing desk

Coordinate Account research specialist · Delivery specialist, Research analyst, Presentation designer for strategy briefing desk.

## Specialist roles

- [Account research specialist · Delivery specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/specialty-3-1-delivery.md): Account brief from supplied public evidence · finished delivery
- [Research analyst](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/research.md): Research and comparisons
- [Presentation designer](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/presentation.md): Presentations and pitches

## Review and handoff

Reviewer: **QA reviewer**. The workflow uses bounded assignments, review and final synthesis. The preset can be saved as a configurable private team; remote updates do not overwrite a user’s saved copy. Tool access and model eligibility remain separate requirements.
