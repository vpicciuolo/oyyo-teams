# Supply chain planning desk

Coordinate Operations specialist, Financial modeler, Mathematics specialist for supply chain planning desk.

## Specialist roles

- [Operations specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/operations.md): Processes and logistics
- [Financial modeler](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/finance.md): Budgets and financial models
- [Mathematics specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/math.md): Mathematics and quantitative analysis

## Review and handoff

Reviewer: **Operations reviewer**. The workflow uses bounded assignments, review and final synthesis. The preset can be saved as a configurable private team; remote updates do not overwrite a user’s saved copy. Tool access and model eligibility remain separate requirements.
