# Project delivery desk

Coordinate Operations specialist, Product strategist, Document specialist for project delivery desk.

## Specialist roles

- [Operations specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/operations.md): Processes and logistics
- [Product strategist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/product.md): Product and business planning
- [Document specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/document.md): Reports, proposals and PDFs

## Review and handoff

Reviewer: **Operations reviewer**. The workflow uses bounded assignments, review and final synthesis. The preset can be saved as a configurable private team; remote updates do not overwrite a user’s saved copy. Tool access and model eligibility remain separate requirements.
