# Sales proposal desk

Coordinate Account research specialist · Delivery specialist, Document specialist, Financial modeler for sales proposal desk.

## Specialist roles

- [Account research specialist · Delivery specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/specialty-3-1-delivery.md): Account brief from supplied public evidence · finished delivery
- [Document specialist](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/document.md): Reports, proposals and PDFs
- [Financial modeler](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-agents/blob/main/agents/finance.md): Budgets and financial models

## Review and handoff

Reviewer: **Marketing reviewer**. The workflow uses bounded assignments, review and final synthesis. The preset can be saved as a configurable private team; remote updates do not overwrite a user’s saved copy. Tool access and model eligibility remain separate requirements.
